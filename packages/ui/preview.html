Visitor H5 · Vant

Booked at the trailhead, checked at the gate

The signboard header and park tiles make the form easy to read and tap outdoors, one-handed. The pass is shaped like a paper permit so staff can check plate, park, date and status from a car window. In China mode the plate comes from a province keyboard. In international mode it is typed in any script, and the phone number carries its country code. Example data.

The same sign, on the staff side

The sidebar and login use the signboard. Element Plus's whole primary ramp comes from the brand token. The four counters share one accent because they are the same kind of number.
